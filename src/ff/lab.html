What the factors earned

How to read it: each line is ₹1 put into one factor at the start and left to compound month by month. Market is the market’s return above cash; size, value and momentum are long-short: small minus big stocks, cheap minus expensive, recent winners minus losers. A line that ends below ₹1 lost money over the years you picked.

A mutual fund’s factor exposures

How to read it: the fund’s monthly return above cash is explained by the factors. A market loading of 1 moves with the market; a positive size loading leans to small companies, value to cheap ones, momentum to recent winners. Alpha is what is left over a year after those exposures. A t above about 2 in size means the number is unlikely to be luck. Fund prices come live from mfapi.in (AMFI NAVs, after fees); direct plans start in 2013. The NIFTY 50 index fund is a check: it can’t beat its index, so its alpha should sit near zero.
